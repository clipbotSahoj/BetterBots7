About BetterBots7

FTC Team 25907, based in Edison, NJ — rookie year 2024, Upper Central League.

BetterBots7 (Team 25907) is a community-supported FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) robotics team based in Edison, New Jersey. Established as a rookie team in 2024, we compete in the New Jersey Upper Central League, focused on designing competitive robots and building youth interest in STEM.

Our goals: designing functional, competitive robots for each year's FTC challenge; growing a passion for engineering, teamwork, and innovation among students and our local community; and practicing Gracious Professionalism — strong character, collaboration, and good sportsmanship — at the regional tournaments we compete in around New Jersey.
